## TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM

### Heading Hierarchy
```
H1: 6xl-8xl | Font-black | Black text | Red accents
H2: 5xl-6xl | Font-bold  | Black text | Premium style
H3: 2xl-3xl | Font-bold  | Black text | Strong presence
H4: xl      | Font-semi  | Black text | Section headers
Body: base  | Font-normal| Gray-700  | Professional
```

### Text Styling
- **All uppercase buttons**: Bold, tracked, red background
- **All uppercase links**: Uppercase, bold, tracked, red hover
- **Star accents**: ★ Used for premium badges and special items
- **Italics**: Used sparingly for philosophy/taglines

---

## INTERACTIVE STATES

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Red bg → Darker red on hover
- **Secondary**: White bg with red border → Red text visible
- **Hover**: Shadow enhancement, color deepening
- **Active**: All caps, bold tracking, white text

### Links
- **Default**: Black or gray text
- **Hover**: Transform to red with smooth transition
- **Duration**: 200ms smooth transition

### Cards
- **Border**: Gray default → Red on hover
- **Shadow**: Subtle → Enhanced on hover
- **Scale**: Slight lift animation (y: -8px)
- **Duration**: 300ms smooth animation
